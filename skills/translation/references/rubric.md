# Translation review rubric

Score each dimension from 1 (unacceptable) to 5 (excellent).

1. **Semantic fidelity** — preserves assertions, negations, modality,
   qualifications, logical connectives, citations, and argumentative force.
2. **Scholastic terminology** — uses stable technical equivalents and does not
   dissolve a term of art into explanatory paraphrase.
3. **Completeness** — omits nothing and adds no commentary to the translation.
4. **English quality** — grammatical and readable for a theology-trained reader
   without making the prose artificially contemporary.
5. **Structural fidelity** — exactly one English block per Latin block; preserves
   headings, emphasis, citations, footnote references, and their order.
6. **Reviewability** — terminology notes identify genuine choices succinctly and
   do not conceal uncertainty.

Reject regardless of total score for an omitted clause, reversed relation,
changed technical claim, invented explanation, or mismatched block count.

Compare candidates blind when practical. Existing English is evidence: retain a
superior established rendering, but correct it when the Latin supports another.
